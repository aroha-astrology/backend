Aroha Astrology connector for Claude

Vedic (Jyotish) astrology inside a Claude conversation: birth charts, kundli matching, panchang, auspicious dates, moon sign horoscopes and numerology, calculated by Aroha's own engine.

What you need

  • A Claude account. Connectors work on claude.ai, the Claude desktop app and the Claude mobile apps.
  • No Aroha account. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to pay for.
  • For a birth chart: the date, time and place of birth. If you do not know the time, a chart is still made, and the result tells you which parts are then unreliable.

Connect it

From the directory. In Claude, open Customize > Connectors, browse the directory, find Aroha Astrology and select Connect.

By address. In Claude, open Customize > Connectors, choose to add a custom connector, and enter this address exactly, with no slash at the end:

https://api.arohaastrology.in/mcp/claude

There is no sign-in step. In a chat, open the + menu, choose Connectors and turn Aroha Astrology on. The first time Claude uses a tool it asks for your permission.

How the results are worked out

Every figure is calculated by Aroha's engine from the positions of the planets, with the Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. Claude explains the result; it does not work out the chart itself.

Places are matched from a built-in list of about 70,000 cities and towns. A very small village may not be on it; give the nearest town instead. When a name could mean more than one place, the tool returns the options so you can pick one.

Readings are traditional astrology offered for reflection. They are not statements of fact and not medical, legal or financial advice. A kundli match is a traditional score, not advice on whether two people should marry.

Your data

  • The connector receives only what a tool needs: birth date, time and place, a name for numerology, or a date and city.
  • These details are used to calculate the answer and are not saved or written to logs. The server records only which tool ran, whether it succeeded and how long it took.
  • A short-lived count of requests per network address is kept to prevent abuse.
  • The connector has no access to an Aroha account. It cannot see, change or delete saved profiles, purchases or anything else in the Aroha app.
  • Place names come from GeoNames, used under the CC BY 4.0 licence. No request leaves Aroha's server to look up a place.

Aroha's full privacy policy is at arohaastrology.in/legal/privacy.

If something goes wrong

  • The place is not found. Add the state or country, or use the nearest larger town.
  • Too many requests. Wait a minute and ask again.
  • The connector will not connect. Check the address has no slash at the end, then remove the connector and add it again.

For anything else, write to us through arohaastrology.in/support.
