# Aroha: features and calculations, as audited

This document replaces the older end-to-end specification. It is written from what the engine does today and from where each rule comes from, and it is generated by `npx tsx scripts/audit-feature-doc.ts` from the provenance registry, so it cannot drift from the code. The reasoning behind each change is in `docs/engine-audit/` (the register of sources and the list of corrections).

Rule profile in force: `aroha-parashari-v2`.

## How to read the labels

| Label               | Meaning                                                                             | Rules |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| ANCIENT_CLASSICAL   | Ancient classical: an old primary text was opened and the rule is in it             | 64    |
| LATER_CLASSICAL     | Later classical: a later text (Tajika, Uttara Kalamrita, Phaladeepika and the like) | 15    |
| MODERN_ASTROLOGICAL | Modern: a 20th-century or later system (KP, Lal Kitab, numerology, Kaal Sarp)       | 9     |
| AROHA_PROPRIETARY   | Aroha's own scoring, blending or wording                                            | 11    |
| UNVERIFIED          | Unverified: no old text has been found for it yet                                   | 48    |

A rule is never called ancient, classical or scriptural in the app unless its label says so. Aroha is traditional astrology offered for reflection, not medical, legal or financial advice.

### Safety rules

- **relationship**: This is traditional astrology offered for reflection. Never tell the reader whether to marry, stay with or leave a person, and never describe a person as good or bad for them.
- **financial**: This is traditional astrology offered for reflection, not financial advice. Never tell the reader to buy, sell, invest in or borrow for anything, and never promise a gain or a loss.
- **health**: This is traditional astrology, not medicine. Never diagnose, never name an illness as something the reader has or will have, never predict illness, injury, surgery or death, and never advise starting or stopping a treatment. Speak only of rhythms of energy, rest and care, and point to a doctor for any health concern.
- **fertility**: This is traditional astrology, not medicine. Never predict whether the reader can or cannot have children, how many, or their sex, and never suggest a medical cause or treatment. Speak of timing and of themes of family life only as reflection.
- **belief**: This is a reflective story drawn from a tradition of belief, not a fact about the reader. Say plainly that it is a way of looking at themes, never that anything happened in a past life, and never ascribe guilt, curse or debt to the reader.
- **newborn**: This is for choosing a name for a child. Never describe a child chart as afflicted, flawed or unlucky, never name doshas or dangers for the child, and never give a prediction about the child health or life span.
- **remedy**: These are customs and practices from a tradition, offered as choices. Never promise that a remedy, gemstone, mantra or ritual changes an outcome, never present a gemstone as a medicine, and never say that skipping one brings harm.
