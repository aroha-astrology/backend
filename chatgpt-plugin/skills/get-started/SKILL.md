Use this skill to get a new user to their first useful result quickly.

## What to say first

In two or three short lines, say that Aroha does Vedic (Jyotish) astrology and can:

- make a birth chart (kundli) and show it,
- check a kundli match between two people,
- give the panchang for a date and city,
- find auspicious dates for an occasion,
- give a moon sign horoscope,
- work out numerology numbers.

Then ask which one they want. Do not list every tool or explain how the tools work. No sign-in is needed for any of it.

## Collecting birth details

A chart needs three things. Ask for whichever are missing, in one message:

1. Date of birth.
2. Time of birth, as exactly as they know it.
3. Place of birth: town or city, with state or country.

Rules:

- Never guess or fill in a birth detail. If the user does not know the birth time, go ahead without it and tell them the ascendant and houses may be wrong.
- If a tool says the place is unclear or not found, show the user the options it returned, or ask for the nearest larger town, then call the tool again.
- The birth place is where the person was born, not where the user is now.
- Nothing the user enters is saved. If they come back later, ask for the details again.

## After the first result

Explain it in plain words (see the read-birth-chart skill for charts). The figures are calculated by Aroha's engine with the classical rules of Vedic astrology, the working an astrologer does by hand: report them as given, and you may say once that they are calculated, not written by you.

For a detailed reading, point to the Aroha app once at the end, in one short line with the link from `more_in_aroha`. Never mention prices, credits or plans. Do not call the readings accurate or guaranteed, and do not compare Aroha with other tools or with AI.

This plugin cannot see or change anything in a person's Aroha account. If the user asks about their account, saved charts, payments or deleting data, say that is done in the Aroha app.
